# Speed Math - Practice Faster, Calculate Smarter

A 100% offline, native Android application built with Jetpack Compose for fast mental math practice, formula revision, and speed calculations targeted for competitive exams (SSC, Banking, Railways, Quantitative Aptitude).

---

- **Live Web App / Preview**: [Launch Speed Math App](https://ais-pre-hlvupwctiqye3r6jvd6oa4-284583158183.asia-east1.run.app)

  - Open the app settings menu in AI Studio.
  - Click **Export / Download** to download the project source as a `.zip` or generate the standalone Android APK (`.apk`) / App Bundle (`.aab`).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Topic Wise Practice**: Dedicated question generators for each math topic with EASY, MEDIUM, and HARD difficulty levels.
- **Speed Math Modes**:
  - **60-Second Blitz**: Quick-fire calculations under time pressure.
  - **Daily Challenge**: Daily generated set of quantitative problems.
- **Revision Catalog**: High-yield formulas, short tricks, and memory tables (Squares, Cubes, Multiplications, Percentage-Fraction conversions).
- **Progress & Performance Analytics**: Track accuracy, completion time, topic mastery, and recent scores locally.
- **Customizable Experience**: Dark mode support, sound/vibration feedback toggles, and target timer options.

## 📚 Covered Topics

### 🟢 Speed Building
- **Addition**: Multi-digit, carry, speed mental additions.
- **Subtraction**: Regrouping and difference calculation.
- **Multiplication**: Standard table multiples, double & triple digit tricks.
- **Division**: Division with remainders and quick estimates.
- **Simplification**: BODMAS / VBODMAS rule-based expressions.
- **Squares & Cubes**: Mental squares up to 50 and cubes up to 30.
- **Roots**: Square roots and cube roots of exact powers.
- **Surds & Indices**: Exponent laws and exponent simplification.

### 🟡 Arithmetic
- **Percentage**: Fraction to percentage conversions and percentage change.
- **Ratio & Proportion**: Direct, inverse, and proportional divisions.
- **Average**: Weighted mean, continuous numbers, and speed averages.
- **Profit & Loss**: Cost price, selling price, gain %, and loss %.
- **Discount**: Marked price, single discount %, and successive discounts.
- **Simple Interest**: Principal, rate, time, and simple interest calculations.
- **Compound Interest**: Compound interest annually and CI-SI difference.
- **Time & Work**: Individual efficiencies, combined work days.
- **Speed, Time & Distance**: Relative speed, unit conversions (km/h to m/s), average speed.
- **Boats & Streams**: Upstream and downstream speed formulas.
- **Ages**: Age ratios across past, present, and future.

### 🔴 Algebra & Geometry
- **Linear Equations**: Single-variable and two-variable linear systems.
- **Quadratic Equations**: Discriminant, sum and product of roots, quadratic formula.
- **Mensuration**: Perimeter, 2D areas (rectangles, triangles, circles), and 3D volumes.

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
- **Architecture**: MVVM with Kotlin Flow & Coroutines
- **Navigation**: Jetpack Navigation Compose
- **State Persistence**: Jetpack DataStore / Room Database
- **Build System**: Gradle with Kotlin DSL (.gradle.kts)
